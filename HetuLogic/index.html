The Philosophy of Anumana

Formulated by the Nyaya school, Anumana (inference) is the process of acquiring knowledge by observing a sign (Hetu) universally related (Vyapti) to an unobserved fact.

The Modern AI Parallel

This structural rigor is the direct ancestor of modern computational logic. What ancient logicians formalized as verbal steps, AI executes as algorithmic validation pipelines.

"Truth is not a modern invention; it is merely rediscovered through new mediums."
