व्याप्ति ⟷ IF-THEN

1. Vyapti (व्याप्ति)

Universal Invariable Concomitance ⟷ Rule-Based Expert Systems
Nyaya Epistemology

Vyapti is the inviolable relation of co-presence between the middle term (Hetu) and major term (Sadhya). "Wherever there is smoke, there is fire" without deviation (Avyabhicharita). It combines inductive observation with empirical falsification tests (Upadhi-shunya).

व्याप्ति = नियतः सहचर-नियमः (Constant Co-presence)
Modern AI & Expert Systems

Knowledge Base Rules & Horn Clauses form the deterministic backbone of symbolic AI. A production rule IF Condition(x) THEN Action(x) or First-Order Logic clause ∀x (P(x) → Q(x)) functions precisely as Vyapti—encoding verified universal implications that fire upon fact unification.

KB Rule: ∀x (Smoke(x) ∧ InvariantLaw → Fire(x))
⚡ Interactive Epistemic Test: Vyapti vs. Upadhi (Conditionality)

Nyaya insists that Vyapti is invalid if an unstated condition (Upadhi) exists. Test the reverse direction:

VALID VYAPTI (व्याप्ति-सत्यम्) Where there is smoke, fire is guaranteed. Counter-examples (Vipaksha like water reservoir) have no smoke.
Domain: Automated Theorem Proving Prolog & Datalog Rete Rule Engines
हेतु ⟷ FEATURE

2. Hetu (हेतु / लिङ्ग)

Logical Indicator / Middle Term ⟷ Feature & Condition Trigger
Nyaya Epistemology

The Hetu (also called Linga or Mark) is the empirical evidence observed on the locus (Paksha). A valid Hetu must fulfill the strict 5-Fold Criterion (Pancha-Rupa Hetu): present in subject, present in positive examples, absent in negative examples, uncontradicted, and uncounterbalanced.

पक्षधर्मता + सपक्षसत्त्व + विपक्षासत्त्व + अबाधित + असत्प्रतिपक्ष
Modern AI & Machine Learning

Input Features & Sensor Activations serve as the algorithmic Hetu. In classification models, decision trees, and neural perceptual layers, a feature trigger (e.g., thermal sensor > 95°C, edge detection gradient) acts as the evidentiary signal that shifts probability distributions or fires deterministic conditional gates.

Trigger: x_feature = f(raw_signal) > Threshold_θ

2 Sapakshasattva Feature present in positive training labels
3 Vipakshasattva-Abhava Feature absent in negative/out-of-distribution cases
4 Abadhitatva Not contradicted by higher-priority sensors

निगमन ⟷ OUTPUT

3. Nigamana (निगमन)

Validated Deduction ⟷ Final Inference Output & XAI Trace
Nyaya Epistemology

Nigamana is the formal restatement of the initial proposition, now crowned with epistemological certainty (Nirnaya). Because all 4 preceding members have passed without fallacy (Hetvabhasa), the conclusion is non-hallucinatory, auditable, and universally communicable (Pararthanumana).

निगमन = सर्वदोष-रहित-प्रमाण-सिद्धान्तः (Validated Truth)
Modern AI & Explainability (XAI)

Certified Inference & Autonomous Action Dispatch. In modern safety-critical AI (avionics, healthcare, autonomous vehicles), raw predictions are insufficient. Nigamana represents the audited, transparent step where the inference engine signs off on a decision with a complete provenance trace.

Output: Commit(State) ∧ Dispatch(Audit_Trace_ID)
💡 Why Nyaya uses 5 Steps vs. Aristotle's 3 Steps

Aristotle’s formal deductive syllogism (Major Premise → Minor Premise → Conclusion) is purely formal and can be factually false (e.g. "All winged things breathe fire"). Nyaya Anumana is both deductive and inductive—it demands an empirical verified exemplar (Udaharana/Drishtanta) and re-grounding (Upanaya) before certifying truth!

Aristotle (Formal Deduction):
All men are mortal → Socrates is a man → Therefore, Socrates is mortal. (Lacks empirical verification clause)
Nyaya (Inductive-Deductive Synthesis):
Pratijna → Hetu → Udaharana (Universal Rule + Kitchen Hearth Exemplar) → Upanaya (Subsumption) → Nigamana (Truth Q.E.D.)

The 4 Valid Pramanas (प्रमाणानि) ⟷ Modern AI Perceptual & Cognitive Layers

In Nyaya epistemology, a Pramana is a valid instrument or faculty of true knowledge (Prama). Without valid Pramanas, any AI system—whether symbolic or deep learning—is prone to ungrounded hallucination.

PRAMANA 01

१. प्रत्यक्ष (Pratyaksha)

Direct Sensory Perception
"इन्द्रियार्थसंनिकर्षोत्पन्नं ज्ञानमव्यपदेश्यमव्यभिचारि व्यवसायात्मकं प्रत्यक्षम्॥"

Knowledge generated from direct contact between senses (Indriyas) and objects. It must be non-verbal, non-deviant, and decisive.

Modern AI Equivalent:

Computer Vision, LiDAR, Audio Spectrum & IoT Telemetry. Raw empirical sensory ingest directly capturing ground-truth environmental state before symbolic abstractions occur.

Sensory Ingestion Pipeline
PRAMANA 02

२. अनुमान (Anumana)

Rational Inference / Deduction
"अथ तत्पूर्वकं त्रिविधमनुमानं पूर्ववच्छेषवत्सामान्यतो दृष्टं च॥"

Knowledge derived from prior perception through the invariant sign (Hetu) and universal rule (Vyapti). Divided into Purvavat, Sheshavat, and Samanyato-drishta.

Modern AI Equivalent:

Automated Deduction, Machine Learning Classifiers, & Rete Rule Engines. Synthesizing unobserved variables from observed features via statistical patterns or formal logic.

Inference & Reasoning Engines
PRAMANA 03

३. उपमान (Upamana)

Analogy & Structural Comparison
"प्रसिद्धसाधर्म्यात्साध्यसाधनमुपमानम्॥"

Knowledge of an unknown object established through its similarity or structural analogy to a known object (e.g. recognizing a wild gavaya because it resembles a domestic cow).

Modern AI Equivalent:

Vector Embeddings, Cosine Similarity, & Few-Shot Learning. Mapping unfamiliar queries to the nearest known vector cluster in high-dimensional latent space (K-Nearest Neighbors).

Verbal Testimony of a Trustworthy Source
"आप्तोपदेशः शब्दः॥"

The verbal or textual testimony of an authentic, verified authority (Apta)—one who possesses accurate knowledge and has no motivation to deceive.

Modern AI Equivalent:

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Cryptographically Signed Oracle Feeds, & Curated Knowledge Bases. Grounding AI responses in verified human databases rather than probabilistic guessing.

The 5 Hetvabhasa (हेत्वाभासाः) ⟷ AI Bugs, Bias & Hallucinations

In Nyaya logic, Hetvabhasa denotes a defective, pseudo-reason that appears valid on the surface but collapses upon scrutiny. Indian logicians cataloged 5 master fallacies—every single one maps directly to a failure mode in modern Machine Learning and LLMs!

FALLACY 01

१. सव्यभिचार (Savyabhichara)

Irregular / Discrepant Reason

The Hetu leads to more than one conclusion; it is not inseparably tied to the Sadhya (e.g., "The mountain has fire because it is knowable"—knowability exists everywhere, even in cold lakes).

AI Failure Mode: Spurious Correlation & Hallucination

A model learns a confounding shortcut (e.g., classifying a husky as a wolf because of snow in the background). The feature is not uniquely causal.

FALLACY 02

२. विरुद्ध (Viruddha)

Contradictory Reason

The Hetu actually proves the exact opposite of what the proposition intended (e.g., "Sound is eternal because it is produced"—production implies destruction).

AI Failure Mode: Data Poisoning & Inverted Gradient

Adversarial attacks or inverted loss functions causing the model to reliably predict the exact inverse of safe operational behavior.

FALLACY 03

३. सत्प्रतिपक्ष (Satpratipaksha)

Counterbalanced Reason

The reason is countered by another equally valid reason proving the opposite proposition (an epistemic tie where both sides hold equal weight).

AI Failure Mode: Conflicting Constraints & Multi-Agent Deadlock

Two safety guardrails or objective functions directly contradict each other, causing LLM output paralysis or decision oscillation.

FALLACY 04

४. असिद्ध (Asiddha)

Unproved / Non-Existent Reason

The Hetu itself has not been established as fact, or the locus does not exist (e.g., "The sky-lotus has fragrance because it is a lotus"—a sky-lotus does not exist).

AI Failure Mode: Out-of-Distribution (OOD) & Unfounded Premises

An inference engine acts on nonexistent sensor telemetry, corrupt input buffers, or fabricated LLM assumptions.

FALLACY 05

५. बाधित (Badhita)

Contradicted by Higher Pramana

The conclusion is directly refuted by a more authoritative Pramana, such as direct sensory perception (e.g., "Fire is cold because it is a substance"—touch instantly refutes it).

AI Failure Mode: Ground-Truth Sensor Override & Safety Tripping

A machine learning prediction attempts to assert that a runway is clear, but direct radar/vision sensors confirm an obstacle, instantly aborting the action.

द्रव्य (Dravya)
Substance / Entity

The substrate in which qualities and actions reside (9 eternal substances: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether, Time, Space, Self, Mind).

Graph Role: Node / Entity (Subject/Object)
Example: Entity(:Server_Node_01) or :Mountain
२
गुण (Guna)
Quality / Attribute

Static properties or values that inhere in substances (24 gunas including color, temperature, dimension, number).

Graph Role: Node Property / Literal Value
Example: { temperature: "98°C", status: "Critical" }
३
कर्म (Karma)
Action / Motion / Event

Dynamic actions causing conjunction or disjunction (upward motion, downward motion, contraction, expansion, locomotion).

Graph Role: State Change Event / Dynamic Edge
Example: :Server_01 -[EMITS_HEAT_SPIKE]-> :Event
४
सामान्य (Samanya)
Generality / Universal / Class

The universal generic essence common to many individuals (e.g., *Cowness* in cows, *Serverhood* in servers).

Graph Role: Ontology Class / `rdf:type` Schema
Example: :Server_01 a :ComputeClusterInstance
५
विशेष (Vishesha)
Particularity / Individuation

The ultimate differentiator that makes an ultimate indivisible atom (Paramanu) unique from another.

Graph Role: Unique Identifier / UUID / Primary Key
Example: UUID("urn:uuid:7c9e6679-7425-40de")
६
समवाय (Samavaya)
Inseparable Inherence / Relation

The eternal, inseparable relation uniting substance and quality, part and whole, motion and actor.

Graph Role: Immutable Structural Edge (`owl:sameAs` / PartOf)
Example: :Thread -[:INSEPARABLE_FROM]-> :Cloth
७
अभाव (Abhava)
Non-existence / Negation

Added by later Vaisheshika masters: the real reality of absence (prior non-existence, posterior destruction, absolute absence, mutual exclusion).

Graph Role: Negation as Failure (NAF) / `NOT EXISTS` Query
Example: NOT EXISTS { ?server :hasCoolantFlow true }
